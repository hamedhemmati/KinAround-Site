Your data & deletion

Draft: the app does not yet have a complete in-app account-deletion flow. This page must be updated with the implemented release flow before launch.

Stop future sharing

Open KinAround on the reporting device and disable its location sharing in the device details. You can also revoke KinAround’s location permission in system settings. Settings changes on other devices may need time and connectivity to sync.

Stopping sharing is not the same as deleting stored data. Previous activity, shared copies, or notification screenshots can remain.

Where your data lives

KinAround uses your private iCloud database for personal records and the circle owner’s shared CloudKit records for circle information. Limited notification signal metadata is also stored in the app’s public CloudKit database. Apple hosts these records, but the app creates and uses them; hosting them in iCloud does not automatically erase them when you stop sharing or uninstall KinAround.

Remove an old device

Open Settings → Manage Devices, choose the obsolete registration, and select Remove Device. If it is your selected personal device, select another device first. Removal disables that registration and attempts to retract its location records from writable circles. A disabled registration marker is retained to prevent that old installation from immediately registering again.

Manage circles and places

Use circle management to leave circles or manage owned circles. Owners can delete a place from its detail screen. These actions do not automatically delete every private record or recipient-held copy associated with your account.

Request account-data deletion

Email help@lowgear.ai with the subject “KinAround data deletion.” We will explain the available removal steps and any identity verification needed. Do not send your Apple Account password.

Some data is in your private iCloud storage and must be removed using an authenticated app flow; the operator cannot assume unrestricted access to that storage. An in-app deletion flow is being prepared for release. Its steps, affected data, and any retained records will be described here when available.

Handle subscriptions separately

Account-data deletion, uninstalling, and signing out do not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel renewal through your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting KinAround data does not delete your Apple Account or unrelated iCloud information.
