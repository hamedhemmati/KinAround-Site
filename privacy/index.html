Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

KinAround helps people share device locations and place notifications within circles they choose to join. This policy explains the information used to provide those features.

KinAround is developed and operated by Hamed Hemmati. For privacy questions, contact help@lowgear.ai.

Location permissions and background use

Location sharing requires device permission and an enabled sharing setting. Always location permission allows the app to receive location updates while it is in the background. You can change permission in the device’s system settings and manage sharing in KinAround.

Disabling sharing or permission stops future location reporting from that device, subject to settings syncing and any operation already in progress. It does not automatically delete previously stored locations or activity.

Who can see shared information

People with access to your circle can see the information shared into that circle, including device locations, names, photos, and place activity. Sharing choices are specific to the circles you select. Private movement-history records are stored separately from shared circle records.

Arrival and departure notifications can display member names, device names, place information, and profile photos on recipients’ devices, including lock screens depending on their notification settings. Recipients may retain screenshots or copies; KinAround cannot remove copies outside its control.

Apple and other service providers

KinAround uses Apple services including iCloud/CloudKit for storage and sharing, MapKit for maps, location services, notifications, Sign in with Apple, and the App Store. Apple processes information under its own privacy policy. Map searches and map interactions may be processed by Apple.

If additional notification or subscription providers are enabled for the release, this policy will identify those providers and the information they process before those features are offered.

Storage and retention

KinAround uses Apple’s CloudKit rather than a separate developer-operated database for its current location and circle features. Personal records, including private movement history, are stored in your private iCloud database. Shared circle records are stored in the circle owner’s CloudKit database and made available to authorized participants. The app also keeps local data on your devices.

To deliver place notifications, KinAround writes notification signal records to the app’s public CloudKit database. These contain opaque recipient tokens and Apple-provided record metadata, such as timestamps and creator identifiers; they do not contain the member’s precise location, name, photo, or place details. Public CloudKit records are separate from your private iCloud database.

Device identity is kept in local storage and a device-local Keychain item to help recognize the same device after reinstalling. Using iCloud does not mean the app stores no data or that its records disappear when the app is uninstalled.

Some activity displays contain a limited recent history, but that does not mean all cloud copies are automatically deleted on the same schedule. Private movement history currently has no automatic deletion schedule. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not, by itself, erase iCloud records.

Use Settings → Delete Account on your iPhone or Watch to remove accessible KinAround account data. A minimal private deletion-state marker remains to prevent previously signed-in devices from recreating a deleted account. Circle-owner-controlled and recipient-held copies may remain. See Your data & deletion for the steps, effects and limits. Support communications may be retained to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations.

Your choices and requests

You can manage device sharing and circle assignments, change system permissions, remove obsolete device registrations, and use the circle management controls available to your role. Owners can edit or delete places. You can contact us about access, correction, or deletion of your information; we may need to verify your identity before acting.

Children and family use

KinAround is intended for people and families. A parent or guardian must supervise a child’s use and provide any consent required by applicable law. Users must meet Apple’s account and service eligibility requirements in their country or region. A family-use purpose or App Store age rating does not remove those requirements.

Location sharing should be used with the knowledge and appropriate consent of the people involved. Parents and guardians should review circle memberships, device permissions, and sharing settings with their children. Do not use KinAround for covert tracking.
