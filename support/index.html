Common questions

How do I add a place?

Open the Map tab and tap an empty location on the map. Name and save the place in the editor. Free circles support three places; at that limit, the app explains the premium upgrade option. Open the Places tab to see a place’s details and activity.

Why is my location not updating?

Check Location Services and KinAround’s location permission on the device that should report. For background sharing, allow Always access. Check connectivity, sharing settings, and the selected personal device. Device settings and operating-system restrictions can delay updates.

Where do I manage my devices?

Open Settings → Manage Devices. Tap a device to view its sharing status and details. Start location sharing or select it as your personal location from KinAround on that device. In free circles, activating another personal device replaces the previous one.

Why do old device entries appear?

Earlier app installations could register again after local app data was cleared. Current versions use device-local Keychain storage to help preserve identity. In Manage Devices, remove obsolete entries. If an old entry is selected as your personal device, choose your current device first. Devices with the same name are not automatically merged.

Why didn’t I receive a place notification?

Check notification permission and Focus settings on the receiving device. Check location permission and sharing on the reporting member’s personal device. Premium place rules may select particular members or transitions. A first location fix establishes a starting state and does not necessarily create an arrival event. Notifications are not guaranteed.

Who gets premium benefits?

Where Premium is offered, the circle owner’s subscription covers members within circles that owner owns. It does not upgrade other owners’ circles. Premium does not bypass device location permissions or sharing choices.

How do I cancel or restore a subscription?

Manage or cancel in your device’s Apple Account subscription settings, or use the subscription-management action in KinAround’s Membership screen. Use Restore Purchases for an existing purchase. Deleting the app or KinAround data does not cancel billing. For refunds, visit Apple’s refund page.

How do I remove my data?

See Your data & deletion. Stopping sharing, signing out, and uninstalling are different from deleting stored iCloud data.
