Free and premium features

The free plan supports one owned circle, up to three active places per circle, and one selected personal location device per member. Joining a circle does not consume an owned-circle allowance.

Where Premium is available, one owner subscription provides premium benefits in circles that subscriber owns. It does not upgrade circles owned by someone else. Premium supports multiple owned circles, up to 20 places per circle, custom place settings and member alerts, and additional enabled devices.
